Kulumai Password Manager Privacy Policy

Welcome to Kulumai Password Manager ("Kulumai", "the App", "we", "our", or "us").

Kulumai is a privacy-first password manager built on a zero-knowledge architecture. Your passwords, secure notes, payment cards, and other confidential information belong exclusively to you.

We do not collect, sell, rent, analyze, or monetize your personal information. We cannot access your encrypted vault or recover your Master Password.

By using Kulumai, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Our Privacy Principles

  • Your data belongs only to you.
  • Encryption happens on your device before any data is stored or synchronized.
  • Your Master Password never leaves your device.
  • We cannot read, decrypt, or recover your vault.
  • We do not operate servers that store your passwords or personal vault data.
  • Cloud synchronization is entirely optional and remains encrypted.

Information You Store

Kulumai allows you to securely store information including, but not limited to:

  • Login credentials
  • Usernames
  • Passwords
  • Website addresses (URLs)
  • Secure notes
  • Payment card information
  • PINs and MPINs
  • Folder names
  • Custom fields
  • Favorites
  • Other information you voluntarily save

This information is encrypted locally on your device before it is stored.

Local Encryption

Kulumai uses strong industry-standard cryptography to protect your data.

  • AES-256 encryption
  • Argon2id password-based key derivation
  • Secure cryptographic keys generated on your device

Encryption and decryption occur only on your device.

Your vault is never transmitted in an unencrypted form.

Master Password

Your Master Password is the foundation of your encrypted vault.

Kulumai uses the Master Password only to derive encryption keys.

  • Is never stored in plain text.
  • Is never transmitted over the Internet.
  • Is never accessible to the developer.
  • Cannot be recovered by the developer.

If you forget your Master Password or Recovery Key (if applicable), your encrypted data cannot be recovered.

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

  • We do not know your Master Password.
  • We cannot decrypt your vault.
  • We cannot access your passwords.
  • We cannot access your secure notes.
  • We cannot access your payment cards.
  • We cannot access your personal information stored within the vault.

Only you possess the information required to decrypt your data.

Data Collection

Kulumai does not collect:

  • Passwords
  • Usernames
  • Notes
  • Payment card information
  • Vault contents
  • Personal files
  • Analytics
  • Usage statistics
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device fingerprinting
  • Marketing information

Kulumai does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Developer Servers

Kulumai does not use proprietary developer servers to store or process your vault.

Your encrypted information remains:

  • On your device, or
  • Within your own cloud storage if you choose to enable cloud synchronization.

Cloud Synchronization (Google Drive)

Cloud synchronization is an optional feature.

  • Your vault is encrypted before it leaves your device.
  • Only encrypted vault data is uploaded.
  • Google Drive stores only the encrypted synchronization file.
  • Neither Google nor the developer can read your vault contents.
  • The synchronization file remains under your Google account.

Kulumai requests only the permissions necessary to create, read, update, and synchronize its own application data within your Google Drive.

The App does not request access to your entire Google Drive.

Google Account Authentication

If you choose to enable Google Drive synchronization, you will be asked to sign in with your Google Account.

This authentication is provided directly by Google using secure OAuth authorization.

Kulumai does not collect, store, or have access to your Google Account password.

Biometric Authentication

Kulumai supports fingerprint and facial authentication using the official Android Biometric API.

Biometric authentication is used solely to unlock your encrypted vault.

  • Never leaves your device.
  • Is never collected by Kulumai.
  • Is never stored by Kulumai.
  • Is never transmitted to the developer.

Kulumai receives only a successful or unsuccessful authentication response from the Android operating system.

Screen Privacy

To help protect your sensitive information, Kulumai enables Android's secure display protections.

  • Screen captures are blocked.
  • Screen recording is prevented.
  • Recent-app preview thumbnails are protected.

This helps reduce accidental exposure of sensitive information.

Android Autofill Service

When Autofill is enabled:

  • Android provides the current application package name or website address so Kulumai can identify matching credentials.
  • This information is used only to provide password suggestions.
  • Kulumai does not continuously monitor your screen.
  • Kulumai does not record screen contents.
  • Kulumai does not log your browsing activity.

Autofill operates only when requested by the Android operating system.

Clipboard Protection

  • The copied information is automatically removed from the clipboard after approximately 30 seconds.
  • On supported Android versions, copied passwords are marked as sensitive to prevent preview notifications from displaying the copied content.

Android Permissions

INTERNET

Used exclusively for optional Google Drive synchronization and Google authentication.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used to notify users about synchronization status, backup completion, and Autofill-related events.

USE_BIOMETRIC

Allows users to unlock their encrypted vault using fingerprint or facial authentication.

BIND_AUTOFILL_SERVICE

Required only if the user enables Android Autofill functionality.

This permission allows Android to request matching credentials for supported applications and websites.

Data Retention

Your encrypted vault remains available until you delete it, uninstall the App, or remove your synchronized vault from Google Drive.

Kulumai does not retain copies of your vault.

Children's Privacy

Kulumai is not intended for children under the age of 18 (or the minimum age required by applicable law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect the application and its security features.

However, no software or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for keeping your Master Password confidential, protecting your Recovery Key (if applicable), and maintaining the security of your device.

Account and Data Deletion Instructions

This app uses Google Sign-In for authentication, but we do not store your profile, passwords, or usage data on any external servers or databases. All of your data remains locally on your device.

Because we do not hold your data on a server, there is no remote account for us to delete. To completely remove your data and sever your connection to this app, please follow these two steps:

  • Delete Local Data: Uninstall the app from your device, or clear the app's storage/cache in your Android settings.
  • Revoke Google Access: Visit your Google Account Security settings at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) and remove this app's access to your Google account.

Your Rights

  • Modify your information.
  • Export your encrypted vault.
  • Delete your vault.
  • Disable cloud synchronization at any time.
